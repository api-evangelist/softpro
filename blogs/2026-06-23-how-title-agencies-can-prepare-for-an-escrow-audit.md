No one looks forward to an escrow audit, but they’re a part of the reality all title companies must navigate. If you’re behind on your reconciliations and documentation, though, an upcoming audit can lead to last-minute scrambles and unnecessary stress. The great news is that getting ready for an escrow audit doesn’t have to be overwhelming.
