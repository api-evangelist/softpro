SoftPro has been named by the Triangle Business Journal as one of its 2026 Best Places to Work. Only 50 companies earned the prestigious 2026 Best Places to Work distinction among hundreds of businesses in the “Triangle” area (Raleigh-Durham-Chapel Hill) across numerous industry sectors, including technology, life sciences, construction, finance, and manufacturing. SoftPro has now been designated among the Triangle’s Best Places to Work 14 times.
